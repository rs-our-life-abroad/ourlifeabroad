# 💳 Banks & Cards for Nomads

**Revolut, Wise, N26... Which cards have no foreign fees? Complete comparison.**

A traditional bank typically charges currency conversion fees (2-3%) and foreign withdrawal fees. Over a year of travel, that quickly adds up to several hundred euros lost.

## 🏦 Neobanks worth knowing

**Revolut** - Currency conversion at the real market rate (within a monthly cap on the free plan), fee-free withdrawals up to a certain amount/month. Handy for daily multi-currency use.

**Wise** - Historically built for low-cost international transfers, also offers a card with real-rate conversion. Very useful if you regularly send/receive money in different currencies.

**N26** - An alternative closer to a traditional bank in how it works, with fee-free options abroad depending on the plan.

## 📋 What we check before choosing a card

- **Monthly free withdrawal cap** (often limited past a certain amount)
- **Exchange rate applied** (real market rate vs a "house" rate with a hidden markup)
- **Possible blocking on unusual activity** - always give your app/bank a heads-up before a big withdrawal in a new country
- **Have a backup card**, ideally from a different provider, in case of a block or loss

## 💰 Potential savings

**~€50/month** in avoided fees compared to a standard French bank card abroad, for regular nomad use.
