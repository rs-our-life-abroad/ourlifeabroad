# 🏥 Long-Term Travel Insurance

**SafetyWing vs Chapka vs WorldNomads. We tested them, here's our verdict.**

Traveling without long-term health/medical evacuation insurance is much too big a gamble - an accident or hospitalization abroad can cost tens of thousands of euros without coverage.

## 🔍 What sets "nomad travel" insurance apart from a standard policy

Standard travel insurance is designed for short stays (2-4 weeks), often with one fixed destination country. For slow travel across several months/years and multiple countries, you need a plan specifically designed for digital nomads, with:

- **Monthly renewal** with no long commitment
- **Multi-country coverage** without having to declare every move
- **A plan you can subscribe to from abroad** (not all of them allow this)

## 🏆 Our choice: SafetyWing

We've used SafetyWing since the start of our trip. Monthly plan with no commitment, health + emergency coverage internationally, 100% online sign-up.

**Price:** around $45/month per person (the exact rate depends on age and chosen coverage zone).

## 📋 Alternatives worth knowing

**Chapka** - French insurer, specific "expat"/long-term plans, useful if you prefer a French-speaking contact.

**WorldNomads** - Well positioned for higher-risk activities (extreme sports, high-altitude trekking...) if your trip includes that kind of activity.

## ⚠️ What you absolutely must check before subscribing

- The **evacuation and hospitalization coverage cap**
- The **exclusions** (risky sports, conflict zones, pregnancy...)
- Any **waiting period** after sign-up
