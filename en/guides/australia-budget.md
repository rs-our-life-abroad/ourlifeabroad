# 🦘 Australia Budget

**8 years of experience: salaries, rent, cost of living... The complete guide.**

Australia is by far the most expensive destination on our journey - but also the one where we could legally work (Working Holiday Visa), which completely changes the budget equation.

## 💰 Overall range

**€2500-3500/month for 2 people**, all-in, for a comfortable city lifestyle.

## 📊 Indicative breakdown (2 people, in a city)

| Item | Monthly range |
|-------|----------------------|
| Accommodation (share house/studio) | €1200-1800 |
| Food | €500-700 |
| Transport | €150-250 |
| Activities & outings | €200-350 |
| Health insurance/Medicare | Depends on visa status |

## 💼 The key point: the WHV lets you work

Unlike Asia or South America where we travel without local income, the Australian Working Holiday Visa lets you legally work on-site. We took on several kinds of jobs (farm work, hospitality, painting, delivery) that largely offset the high cost of living.

## 📍 What makes the budget vary

- **Sydney/Melbourne** are notably more expensive than the rest of the country, especially for housing
- **Rural areas/farms** (often necessary to qualify for the 2nd year WHV) have a much lower cost of living, sometimes with accommodation included with the job
- **Perth and Western Australia**, where we spent a good chunk of our time, stay more affordable than the East Coast
