# 👕 Clothing & Minimalism

**How to live with a 40L backpack? Our complete clothing list + organization tips.**

Traveling light isn't just about physical comfort - it's also what lets you stay mobile (changing accommodation, catching a local bus, climbing stairs with no elevator) without wrecking your back.

## 🧺 The "capsule wardrobe" rule

The idea: few pieces, but ones that all mix and match and work across multiple climates.

- **3-4 technical tops** (quick-dry, odor-resistant) rather than regular cotton
- **2 convertible pants/shorts** that go from hiking to city wear
- **1 packable warm layer** (light down jacket or fleece) even for tropical destinations - air-conditioned buses and altitude always catch you off guard
- **1 lightweight waterproof jacket**

## 🧦 What we often underestimate

Technical socks and underwear (merino or quick-dry synthetic) are well worth the price: they dry overnight after a hand wash, unlike cotton.

## 🧼 Handling laundry on the road

You don't need a laundromat everywhere: a small tube of concentrated detergent + a foldable clothesline is enough to hand-wash in most accommodations. For bigger loads, local laundromats (often charged by weight) stay very affordable in most of the countries we've traveled through.

## ⚖️ Target weight

**8-10kg per person**, full luggage included (not counting the backpack itself). Beyond that, you start feeling the difference on transport and stairs.
