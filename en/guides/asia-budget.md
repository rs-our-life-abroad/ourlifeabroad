# 🌏 Southeast Asia Budget

**Cost of living in Thailand, Bali, Vietnam? Detailed budgets by city.**

Southeast Asia remains one of the most accessible regions for slow travel, with an excellent value-for-money ratio on accommodation and local food.

## 💰 Overall range

**€800-1500/month for 2 people**, all-in (accommodation, food, local transport, activities, insurance).

## 📊 Indicative breakdown (2 people)

| Item | Monthly range |
|-------|----------------------|
| Accommodation (studio/apartment) | €300-500 |
| Food (mix local market/restaurant) | €250-450 |
| Local transport (scooter, tuk-tuk, bus) | €60-100 |
| Activities & sightseeing | €100-250 |
| Health insurance | €90 (~$45/person) |

## 📍 What makes the budget vary

- **Bali (Ubud)** is generally the most affordable option in the region for a couple doing slow travel
- **Bangkok/Chiang Mai** stay very accessible outside touristy areas
- **Vietnam** offers excellent value for money, especially on street food
- **Highly touristy islands** (some areas of the Philippines, Phuket...) drive up accommodation costs

## 💡 How to cut your budget

- Cook at home for lunch, save restaurants for dinner
- Book accommodation by the month rather than by the night (see our [accommodation guide](/en/guides/finding-accommodation/))
- Prefer long-term scooter rentals over daily rentals
