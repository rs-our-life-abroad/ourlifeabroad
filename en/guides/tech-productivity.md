# 📱 Tech & Productivity

**MacBook, iPhone, action cameras... What gear for creating content while traveling?**

Beyond choosing the right devices (which we cover in our [Nomad Setup article](/en/guides/nomad-setup/)), staying productive on the road is mostly about good digital organization.

## ☁️ Backup and storage

Losing your photos/videos or your laptop on the road isn't an option. Simple rule: **the 3-2-1 rule** - 3 copies of your files, on 2 different media, with 1 off-site (cloud). We systematically sync to a cloud (Google Drive, iCloud or similar) as soon as we have decent wifi.

## 🗂️ Organization tools

- **Notion or Google Docs** to centralize article ideas, budgets, to-do lists
- **Offline Google Maps** for every destination (download the map BEFORE arriving, connection isn't always guaranteed)
- **A password manager** (Bitwarden, 1Password...) - essential with all the accounts you juggle while traveling

## 🔌 The small gear that changes everything

- **Universal power strip/adapter** with built-in USB-C ports
- **20,000mAh+ power bank** for long filming/work days
- **External SSD** for physical backups on top of the cloud

## 💰 Investment

**€2500-4000** all-in (gear + cloud/tool subscriptions over the year). It can seem high, but it's an investment that lasts several years of travel if the gear is well maintained.
