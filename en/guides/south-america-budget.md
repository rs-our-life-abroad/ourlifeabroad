# 🌎 South America Budget

**Argentina, Paraguay, Peru... Real monthly expenses, item by item.**

This is our current playground (2025-2026). South America is generally pricier than Southeast Asia, but stays very accessible compared to Western Europe or Australia.

## 💰 Overall range

**€1200-2000/month for 2 people**, all-in.

## 📊 Indicative breakdown (2 people)

| Item | Monthly range |
|-------|----------------------|
| Accommodation (studio/apartment) | €400-700 |
| Food (mix market/restaurant) | €350-550 |
| Local transport | €80-150 |
| Activities & sightseeing | €150-300 |
| Health insurance | €90 (~$45/person) |

## 📍 What makes the budget vary

- **Argentina**: local inflation makes prices very volatile - the budget in euros/dollars can swing a lot month to month depending on the exchange rate
- **Paraguay (Asunción)**: one of the cheapest countries in the region, particularly low accommodation costs
- **Peru (Lima, Arequipa)**: average cost of living, but excellent, affordable food scene (ceviche, local markets)

## 💡 How to cut your budget

- In Argentina, check the most favorable exchange options at the time of your stay (the situation shifts fast)
- Eat at local markets rather than touristy downtown restaurants
- Prefer long-distance buses (often comfortable and affordable) over flights for internal trips
