# 🔐 Security & VPN

**Protect your data on public WiFi. NordVPN, ExpressVPN... Our comparison.**

Between airport WiFi, cafés, and Airbnb accommodations, we connect to dozens of public networks a year. A minimum of precautions is a must, especially for online payments and banking access.

## 🛜 Why a VPN while traveling

A VPN encrypts your connection, which stops someone on the same public WiFi network from intercepting your data (passwords, banking info). It's also useful to access services under geo-restriction in certain countries.

## 🏆 The VPNs we use/recommend

**NordVPN** - Fast, good server network, simple interface. Our main pick.

**ExpressVPN** - Solid alternative, particularly reliable in countries with heavy internet censorship.

## 📋 Our basic security rules

- **Never make a bank payment on public WiFi without a VPN active**
- **Two-factor authentication (2FA)** on all sensitive accounts (bank, email, social media)
- **A password manager** rather than reused passwords
- Avoid open public WiFi networks for sensitive admin tasks (visa applications, banking)
