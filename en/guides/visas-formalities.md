# ✈️ Visas & Formalities

**Tourist visa, visa run, e-visa... Everything you need to know by country.**

⚠️ **Important:** visa rules change regularly depending on the country and your nationality. This guide gives a general method - always check official sources (embassy/consulate or government website) before you leave, we can't guarantee country-specific info stays up to date in real time.

## 🗂️ The main categories to know

**Visa on arrival** - The simplest, often free or low-cost, usually valid 15 to 90 days depending on the country.

**e-Visa** - Applied for online before departure, usually processed within a few days. More and more countries are moving to this system instead of paper visas at the embassy.

**Visa run** - Leaving the country and coming back to "reset" the allowed stay duration. Historically common in Southeast Asia, but increasingly regulated/restricted by local authorities - check case by case, it's not a guaranteed long-term solution.

**Working Holiday Visa (WHV)** - For ages 18-30/35 (depending on bilateral agreements), lets you travel AND work legally in the country for 1 year, sometimes renewable. That's what we used for our initial stay in Australia.

## 📋 Our checklist before every new country

- Check the **allowed stay duration** for your specific nationality (not just "in general")
- Anticipate the **total cost** (visa fees + possible exit fees from the previous country)
- Check whether a **passport valid 6 months after the return date** is required (a common rule)
- Keep a **digital copy** of all your documents (cloud + email to yourself)
