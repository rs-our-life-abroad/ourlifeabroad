# 🇦🇺 Australia

**The starting point of our whole adventure.**

We left for a 1-year Working Holiday Visa in 2018, and ended up staying 7 years. A year-long road trip across the country (Perth, Busselton, Margaret River, Karijini, Shark Bay, Exmouth, Broome, Darwin, Uluru, Port Augusta, Adelaide, Toowoomba, Brisbane, Sydney...), followed by several years living there between odd jobs (farm work, hospitality, painting, delivery...) and discovering a completely different way of life.
