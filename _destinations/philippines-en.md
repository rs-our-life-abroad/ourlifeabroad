# 🇵🇭 Philippines

**An archipelago we've only scratched the surface of, and it made us want to come back.**

Over 7,000 islands means we've only touched a fraction of what the country has to offer. Beaches, diving, and a local kindness that stays with you.

Less cheap than neighboring Vietnam or Thailand in some ways (inter-island transport especially), but still very accessible for slow travel.
