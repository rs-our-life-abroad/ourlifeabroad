# 🇵🇪 Arequipa, Peru

**Nicknamed "the White City" for its colonial buildings made of sillar (light-colored volcanic stone).**

Arequipa stands out for its UNESCO-listed historic center and its spectacular setting, surrounded by several volcanoes including the imposing Misti.

A calmer vibe than Lima, and a great base for treks to Colca Canyon.
