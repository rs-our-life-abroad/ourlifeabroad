# 🇦🇷 Buenos Aires, Argentina

**The starting point of our Argentine leg.**

Buenos Aires surprises with its very European architecture (sometimes called "the Paris of South America"), its neighborhoods with wildly different vibes (San Telmo, Palermo, Recoleta), and a café culture that invites you to slow down.

A city we took our time exploring before heading off to Salta and Argentina's Northwest.
