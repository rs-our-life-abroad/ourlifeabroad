# 🇵🇾 Asunción, Paraguay

**The South American country nobody talks about, and that's a shame.**

Asunción isn't on most classic South America itineraries, which is exactly what makes it a place where you'll run into very few tourists. Laid-back vibe, some of the lowest cost of living in the region, and its own cultural identity (Guaraní is spoken everywhere, alongside Spanish).

One of the bets of our current trip: going off the beaten path.
