# 🇮🇩 Bali - Island of the Gods

**2 months living like locals between rice paddies and temples.**

## 🏠 Where We Stayed

**Ubud** - Bali's cultural heart. We rented a small apartment near the Sacred Monkey Forest.

**Accommodation budget:** €400/month for a nice studio with shared pool.

**The good tip:** Skip Canggu if you're not a surfer. It's become super touristy and expensive. Ubud stays authentic.

---

## 💰 Real Budget (2 people, 2 months)

| Category | Monthly cost |
|-----------|--------------|
| Accommodation | €400 |
| Food (mix restaurant/home) | €450 |
| Scooter rental | €60 |
| Coworking | €0 (worked from the apartment) |
| Sightseeing & activities | €150 |
| **TOTAL** | **€1060/month** |

**That's ~€17/day/person** - Really accessible!

---

## 🍜 Our Favorite Food Spots

**Warung Biah Biah** (Ubud)
Nasi campur for 25k IDR (~€1.50). The best value on the island.

**Sage** (Ubud)
For a healthy brunch after a week of nasi goreng. Pricey for Bali (€10) but worth it.

**Ubud Night Market**
Local street food every night. We went 3-4 times a week.

---

## 📱 Practical Life

**Internet:** 100mbps fiber for €20/month. Perfect for remote work.

**Local SIM:** Telkomsel, €15/month for unlimited data.

**Transport:** Scooter is a MUST. Grab Bike for lazy days (€1-2 a ride).

---

## 🎯 What We Loved

✅ The kindness of Balinese people (genuinely, not just for tourists)
✅ Tegallalang rice terraces at sunrise
✅ The ultra-affordable cost of living
✅ The digital nomad scene (we made friends for life)
✅ Authentic warungs everywhere

---

## 😅 What We Liked Less

❌ Denpasar traffic (hell)
❌ Monsoon rain in November (soaked every other day)
❌ The Monkey Forest monkeys that stole our sunglasses
❌ The classic scam: "temple closed today, I'll take you somewhere else" (FALSE, always)

---

## 💡 Our Tips

**Best period:** April to October (dry season)

**Recommended length:** Minimum 3 weeks to really enjoy it

**Daily budget:** €20-30/person if living local, €50-70 for more comfort

**Visa:** 30 days free on arrival, extendable once locally
