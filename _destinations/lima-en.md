# 🇵🇪 Lima, Peru

**The capital that turned Peru into a global food destination.**

Lima is a sprawling city on the Pacific coast, known for its culinary scene (ceviche leading the way) and its very contrasting neighborhoods, from Miraflores to Barranco.

A key stop on our South American trip, between memorable meals and full immersion in a Peruvian megacity.
